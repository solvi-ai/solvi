# `solvi`

The names exported by the package: the entry points `build` and `Guard`, `Budget`, and the vocabulary every part of
solvi shares — the catalog, questions and answers, `System` and `Response`, quotes, claims, decisions, `Fail`,
`Unknown`, the typed answers and the two warnings. The rest is imported from its module: `solvi.core` (the low level),
`solvi.models`, `solvi.solutions.*`, `solvi.experimental.*`.
